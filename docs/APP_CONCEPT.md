# App Concept: Inbound Deal Triage

## Problem
Talent managers and creator coordinators live in crowded inboxes. Brand briefs arrive as long, forwarded, multi-person threads with attachments and changing terms. Deliverables, dates, usage, fees, whitelisting, and exclusivity are buried across messages. Teams manually copy terms into spreadsheets, compare rate cards from memory, chase missing information, and risk accepting underpriced work or hidden category conflicts.

## Solution
Creator Signal OS creates a trusted deal workspace from each inbound thread. It preserves the source and latest terms, extracts a structured brief, marks ambiguity, matches the right creator and rate card, flags whitelisting/exclusivity and other redlines, and generates an editable counter-offer. It accelerates judgment rather than replacing it.

## Users
Talent managers and creator coordinators managing multiple creators, agencies, and brand relationships. They are fast, commercially experienced, email-centric, and need a prioritized queue with explainable answers.

## Core flows
1. Configure organization, members, creators, contacts, rate cards, and redline rules.
2. Connect a forwarding address/webhook through Resend or Postmark.
3. Receive a thread; verify, deduplicate, normalize, and queue it.
4. Review extracted brief fields, evidence spans, confidence, and missing information.
5. Select or confirm creator; compare proposed fee and usage to the active rate card.
6. Inspect risk cards for exclusivity, whitelisting, paid media, perpetual usage, cancellation, payment, and category conflicts.
7. Generate, edit, copy, and approve a counter-offer. Sending remains explicit and external to the autonomous pipeline.

## UX philosophy
Minimal Neo-Swiss: 1px borders, crisp type, compact tables, high information density, neutral palette with restrained red/amber/green risk accents, obvious source provenance, keyboard shortcuts, responsive split-pane triage, and no ornamental gradients or heavy shadows.

## Value
Turn an hour of thread archaeology into a few minutes of confident review, protect creator economics and relationships, standardize negotiation quality, and create an auditable history of every decision.

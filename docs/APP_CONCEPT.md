# RosterOps Product Manifesto

Talent managers are human middleware between creators, brands, agencies, emails, contracts, deliverables, calendars, invoices, travel, and reporting. A passive CRM records this work after the fact. RosterOps is an active AI workforce that turns intent and messy inputs into coordinated execution while preserving the manager's judgment and relationships.

## Wedge
Send one messy deal: a thread, PDF, or forwarded brief becomes a campaign workspace with extracted terms, creator fit, redlines, kickoff, brand drafts, deliverable deadlines, approval tracking, invoice timing, travel/logistics prompts, and reporting checklist.

## Workforce
Inbox/Comms handles Gmail/Outlook understanding, manager-voice drafts, and action items. Deal handles opportunities, rates, usage, exclusivity, redlines, and negotiation history. Campaign runs deliverables, deadlines, approvals, posting, brand comms, and reporting. Sales/Outreach provides Apollo-style brand intelligence, decision-makers, warmth, intent, and pitches. Creator maintains goals, audience, pillars, stats, category and whitelisting preferences. Finance handles invoices, Net 30/60, splits, payments, and forecasts. Career/Strategy handles trends, fit, and growth. Logistics handles calendars, shoots, travel, events, and itineraries.

## Products
Manager Workspace: an individual manager runs 5–20 creators with a dedicated AI team, prioritized work queue, shared memory, and approval inbox. Agency Command Center: leadership sees managers, rosters, pipeline, aggregate revenue, workload, risk, and cross-agency memory with strict permissions.

## User promise
Never lose a brief, deadline, approval, invoice, relationship nuance, or creator preference in the cracks between systems. RosterOps makes the agency's invisible operating work legible and executable.

## Grand vision
AI-native talent-agency infrastructure: Creator <-> AI Representation Layer <-> Brand / Castlane. Begin with post-sale operations and inbound triage; expand into proactive sales, career strategy, finance, logistics, and a trusted representation network.
